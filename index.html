⚠️ URGENT PUBLIC WARNING - PROTECT YOURSELF

The Ministry of Home Affairs, Cyber Crime Division, issues this critical warning to all citizens regarding Instagram phishing scams. The arrested individual is part of a larger network still operating.

🚫 NEVER Share These Details:

  • OTP (One-Time Password) - No legitimate organization will ask for OTP
  • Bank Account Details - Account number, IFSC code, CVV, PIN
  • Aadhaar Number - Unless on official government portals
  • PAN Card Details - Keep confidential at all times
  • Credit/Debit Card Information - Never share CVV or expiry date
  • Net Banking Passwords - Banks never ask for passwords
  • Social Media Passwords - No one should ask for these

🔍 How to Identify Phishing Attempts:

  • Messages claiming you won a lottery you never entered
  • Urgent requests for personal information or money
  • Suspicious links asking you to "verify" your account
  • Fake government scheme notifications
  • Impersonation of celebrities or officials
  • Too-good-to-be-true offers and prizes
  • Messages with spelling errors or poor grammar
  • Requests to download unknown apps or files

✅ Safety Measures to Follow:

  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on all accounts
  • Verify sender identity before responding to messages
  • Check official websites directly, don't click links in messages
  • Keep your devices and apps updated
  • Use strong, unique passwords for each account
  • Be skeptical of unsolicited messages
  • Report suspicious accounts immediately
  • Educate family members, especially elderly

⚠️ REMEMBER: Government agencies, banks, and legitimate companies will NEVER ask for sensitive information via Instagram, WhatsApp, or SMS. Always verify through official channels.

📝 How to File a Complaint:

  1. Visit cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930
  2. Provide your personal details (Name, Mobile, Email)
  3. Describe the incident with date, time, and details
  4. Upload evidence (Screenshots, messages, transaction details)
  5. Submit the complaint and note your complaint number
  6. Track your complaint status online

📸 Evidence to Collect:

  • Screenshots of suspicious messages/profiles
  • Bank transaction details and statements
  • Phone numbers and email addresses used by scammers
  • URLs of phishing websites
  • Date and time of incidents
  • Any communication records
